Backdrop photograph
  cosmic-cliffs-carina.png        <- carina_nebula~medium.jpg
  NASA / ESA / CSA / STScI, James Webb Space Telescope NIRCam image of the
  "Cosmic Cliffs" in the Carina Nebula.
  NASA Image and Video Library: https://images.nasa.gov/details/carina_nebula
  Downloaded from https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/carina_nebula/carina_nebula~medium.jpg

Rejected candidate, kept for comparison
  orion-infra.png                 <- PIA25434~medium.jpg
  NASA/JPL-Caltech, Orion Nebula in infrared.
  Too murky at the brightness the map needs; the Carina frame keeps a dark blue
  star field above the clouds, which is what a backdrop wants.

Processing (all in the script, reproducible):
  crop 1280x640 from the top of the frame, scale to 1024x512, two box-blur passes
  at radius 4, brightness 0.75, gamma 2.0, vignette 0.55.
